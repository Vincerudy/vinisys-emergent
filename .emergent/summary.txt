<product_requirements>
The primary objective was to stabilize and enhance the Vinisys payroll application. Key features revolved around a comprehensive Notes de frais module:
1.  **Notes de frais as an Envelope:** Each note contains multiple individual expenses (Frais).
2.  **Frais Creation/Editing:** A dedicated sidebar interface for manual entry of expense details (vendor, date, amount) and a Type de frais dropdown.
3.  **Note Details Page:** A page to view a specific expense note, list its contained Frais, and display a dynamic total amount.
4.  **Submission Workflow:** A Soumettre button to change note status to Soumise with a confirmation modal.
5.  **Expense Note List:** A table view of all expense notes with filtering and links to detail pages.
6.  **Configuration:** A Paramétrage page to manage Type de frais.
7.  **Validation:** A Validation notes page for managers to validate submitted notes.

Additionally, a Rapport page was requested to display real-time financial data:
1.  **Key Metrics:** Total revenue, number of invoices, collected vs. pending amounts, total credits issued, TVA distribution.
2.  **Expense Metrics:** Total expenses, reimbursed expense notes, categorization of expenses, HT/TTC/recoverable VAT.
3.  **Net Profit:** Calculation based on collected revenue, credits, total expenses (minus recoverable VAT), and reimbursed expense notes. All data must be real, from the database.
</product_requirements>
